# Qiskit Fall Fest KNUST 2026 — Hackathon Track

Welcome to the **KNUST Quantum Hackathon** track of the Qiskit Fall Fest KNUST 2026 (November 02 – November 06, 2026).

## Challenge Tracks
1. **Quantum Algorithm Implementation & Optimization**
2. **Quantum Machine Learning & Chemistry Applications**
3. **Open Innovation for African Science & Technology**

## Problem Prompts
Explore our curated set of 10 challenge prompts in the [`PROMPTS/`](../PROMPTS) folder:
- **Foundational & Intermediate Challenges (01–08):** Sourced and adapted from **QuantumKE** (Qiskit Fall Fest Nairobi 2026).
- **Advanced Domain Challenges (09–10):** Quantum Chemistry (VQE) and Hybrid Quantum-Classical Generative AI, developed by the KNUST organizing team.

## Submission Guidelines
- Work individually or in teams of 1 to 4 participants.
- Structure your project repository clearly with well-documented Jupyter notebooks or Python source files.
- Refer to [`PROMPTS/JUDGING_CRITERIA.md`](../PROMPTS/JUDGING_CRITERIA.md) for detailed scoring criteria (maximum 110 points).
- Review [`PROMPTS/AI_POLICY.md`](../PROMPTS/AI_POLICY.md) for transparent and effective use of AI tools.
