# 💡 Hackathon Challenge Prompts — Qiskit Fall Fest KNUST 2026

Welcome to the hackathon challenges for **Qiskit Fall Fest KNUST 2026** (November 02 – November 06, 2026), hosted by the Qiskit KNUST Student Chapter in the Department of Physics, KNUST.
