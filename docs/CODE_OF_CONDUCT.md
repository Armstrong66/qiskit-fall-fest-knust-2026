# Qiskit Fall Fest KNUST — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all participants, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or level of quantum experience.

## Standards & Expectations
- Be respectful, collaborative, and constructive during discussions, workshop sessions, and hackathon teamwork.
- Exercise empathy and patience when mentoring newcomers to Python, quantum computing, and Qiskit.
- Honor academic integrity and acknowledge sources in hackathon projects.

## Reporting & Contact
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the lead organizers immediately at `qiskit.knust@gmail.com` or reach out to a student chapter lead on Discord.
